The origin

Go anyway.

I've been obsessed with the ocean for as long as I can remember. Not in a vague, aesthetic way — actual obsession, the kind you have as a kid before you learn to be embarrassed about it. Octopuses specifically.

There's a spot a mile down where basically nothing should be able to survive, and not only does something survive down there, it's smart. It solves problems. It changes its whole body to fit through a gap the size of its own eye. It never had to become normal to make it.

That sticks with me living in a city where the easiest way to get through a day is to look like everybody else.

The ocean doesn't owe you anything. It won't tell you what you'll find before you go in, and it won't reward you for being brave about it. That's actually the point — nobody down there is deciding whether you're allowed. You just go, or you don't.

The anchor's on the chest because you should know what you are before you go looking for what's dark. Heavyweight cotton because it should last past one season, not just look good in it.

Small runs. No investors. Not selling this, ever.

The water doesn't care if you made it through. You did anyway — that's the wake.
