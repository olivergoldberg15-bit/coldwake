Our Story

Made for the deep end.

Coldwake is a study in pressure, movement, and calm below the surface. Built for the moments that pull you beyond the shoreline, with fewer pieces and a sharper point of view.

One tee, cut heavy and built to last. We start small and make it right — no noise, no filler, just the pieces we'd wear every day off the dock.
